## 7. Font Note

`assets/graffiti-constructor-pixel-art/1 Font` contains loose per-character glyph PNGs without font metrics or kerning data. As instructed, we bypass it and use Godot's built-in font rendering with clean bold outlines and shadows for high-readability arcade fighter presentation.
